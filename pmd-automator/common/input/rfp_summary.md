# Request for Proposal (RFP): SmartLife Mobile Health & Wellness Platform

**Issuer:** TechVision Global Inc.  
**Issue Date:** 09 May 2026  
**Submission Deadline:** 23 May 2026  
**Format:** PDF via Email  

## A. Background & Context
TechVision Global Inc. aims to bridge a digital gap. Currently, the company's digital transformation rate is 10%, lagging behind industry peers. The SmartLife app is the flagship initiative to modernize offerings for Millennials and Gen Z in North America and Europe.

## B. Scope of Services
Delivery of a native iOS (Swift) and Android (Kotlin) application integrated with a Node.js / MongoDB backend.
* **Wearable Integration:** Syncing from Apple Watch (iOS) and Android-based smartwatches.
* **AI Risk Prediction:** Analytics engine for health risks based on vitals.
* **Social Connectivity:** Community and sharing tools to engage the target demographic.

## C. Vendor Requirements
* **Specific Experience:** Track record in health-tech handling biometric data.
* **Case Studies:** At least three examples of live apps featuring AI/ML integration.
* **Compliance Knowledge:** Experience with healthcare data privacy standards (HIPAA, GDPR).

## D. Evaluation Criteria
* **Technical Expertise:** Native development proficiency (Swift and Kotlin).
* **AI Model Robustness:** Quality of the proposed health-risk prediction model.
* **Timeline & Budget:** Ability to deliver within a 24-week timeline and $450,000 budget.
